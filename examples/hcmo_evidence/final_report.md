# Dark-phase locomotor activity

## Research question

Does mouse 1 maintain mean dark-phase locomotor activity above 100 activity
counts per hour under limited human interaction?

## Finding

The exported observation had a mean of 143.2 activity counts per hour. This
supports the prespecified hypothesis for this subject and interval only; it is
not a population-level effect estimate.

## Limitations

This synthetic prototype contains one subject, one enclosure, one sensor, and
one observation interval.
